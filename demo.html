Act I

Day One.
You need prod access.

Every engineer has lived this. You have a bug. You need logs. You ask how to get prod access and receive a 47-step wiki page last updated when the company had 40 people. You now have 280.

9AM
Post in #sre-access-requests

⏳ Waiting...

📅 3 weeks out
Day 22

❌ Too late

Act II

The Policy.
January 2026.

The wiki had all the answers. The wiki was very confident about this. The wiki had not been told about the April incident review.

access-policy.md · effective January 2026

  1. Engineer opens a ticket in #sre-access-requests.
  2. Engineer tags their direct manager AND the SRE on rota for that week.
  3. SRE schedules a 30-minute pairing session within 2 working days.
  4. After pairing, SRE files access via Okta. Provisioning in ~4 hours.

Tenure requirement: 2 weeks minimum.
Policy reviewed quarterly. Last review: 2025-12-15.

DEPRECATED
